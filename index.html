Put Lazeeza on your Home Screen

  1. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ (bottom of Safari, or ⋯ first).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. If you see Open as Web App, switch it off. It opens in Safari, where you're already signed in.
  4. Tap Add. Then delete the old Lazeeza icon.
Open Lazeeza now

Keep this icon to yourself. It holds your app’s private key.
