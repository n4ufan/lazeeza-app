Put Lazeeza on your Home Screen

  1. Tap the Share button (bottom of Safari — tap ⋯ first if you don’t see it).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App switched ON.
  4. Tap Add. Then delete the old Lazeeza icon.

Keep this icon to yourself — it holds your app’s private key.

Install Lazeeza

Its own icon, full screen, no Google bar at the top.

  1. Tap ⋮ at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app — or Add to Home screen ▸ Install.
  3. Open Lazeeza from your Home screen. Then delete any old Lazeeza icon or bookmark.

Samsung Internet: tap ☰ ▸ Add page to ▸ Home screen.

Installed. Open Lazeeza from your Home screen.

Keep this to yourself — it holds your app’s private key.
